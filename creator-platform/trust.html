TRUST & SAFETY

Protect creators without hiding the process.

INKSIDE is designed around original work, clear creator rights and human review. Reports create a review queue; they do not automatically punish or remove a creator.

Original work first

Creators confirm they own the work or have permission to publish it before posting. Impersonation, knowingly stolen uploads and deceptive attribution are not allowed.

Context before takedowns

A report is a signal for review, not a verdict. We look at the work, the report reason and available context before restricting content or accounts.

Mature content labels

Creators can mark work as mature or sensitive. Labeling helps people choose what they see and gives moderators a clearer first signal during review.

Appeals matter

If moderation removes or limits a work, the creator should receive the reason and a route to request another review. Repeated abuse of reporting tools may itself be actioned.

How a report moves through INKSIDE

1
Report submitted

A viewer chooses a reason such as stolen work, harassment, spam, mature-content labeling or another policy concern.

2
Queued for review

The work stays visible by default unless there is a credible urgent safety concern.

3
Moderator decision

The reviewer can take no action, add/adjust a label, limit distribution, remove the work or escalate the account.

4
Creator informed

When action is taken, the creator should receive the policy reason and an appeal path.

Production note: the current MVP stores demo reports locally in the browser. Before public transactions are enabled, moderation queues, evidence storage and creator notifications need a durable authenticated backend.
