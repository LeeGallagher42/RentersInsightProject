CREATOR GUIDE

Publish clearly. Price fairly. Keep 90%.

The creator experience should make the important decisions obvious: what you are publishing, what buyers receive and exactly how much of each sale comes back to you.

From draft to published

1
Choose art or comic

Set a title, description, tags and — for comics — page count so discovery can route the work correctly.

2
Choose free or paid

Free work can build audience. Paid work shows the 90/10 creator split before publishing.

3
Confirm rights and labeling

Confirm ownership/permission, community-guideline compliance and mature-content labeling where needed.

4
Publish and learn

Work appears in discovery and your profile. Analytics events are structured for future dashboards without requiring tracking libraries in the MVP.

Before you hit publish

✓ The title and cover make sense without extra context.
✓ The description tells buyers what they are receiving.
✓ Tags describe the work rather than stuffing unrelated keywords.
✓ You own the work or have permission to publish it.
✓ Sensitive or mature work is labeled appropriately.
✓ For paid work, you are happy with the displayed creator payout.

Production payments, tax handling and payout schedules require final terms and payment-provider integration. The 90/10 split is the platform positioning, not a promise that payment-processing fees or taxes disappear.
