Creator help

From upload to audience.

A compact guide to how the creator-facing MVP works today and what the 90/10 promise means.

01

Prepare your work

Use a clear title, description, relevant tags, and a content note when people should know something before opening the work. Comics can be organised page-by-page before publishing.

02

Confirm your rights

Publishing requires confirmation that you own or control the rights to share the work and that it follows the community rules.

03

Publish into discovery

Published work can appear in searchable discovery, creator profiles and your Creator Studio catalogue. Mature work remains hidden by default unless viewers opt in.

90%

Your revenue share

Creators keep 90% of platform sale revenue. INKSIDE keeps 10%. Payment processing and applicable taxes are intended to be shown separately before live checkout.

04

Understand signals

Likes, saves, follows, shares and checkout intent are already structured as analytics events so a real backend can ingest them later without redesigning the product surface.

05

Moderation

Reports enter a review queue rather than automatically removing work. The MVP includes moderation states so server-side review and appeals can slot in next.
