Community safety

Make boldly. Treat people decently.

INKSIDE is for original art and comics. These baseline rules are designed to protect creators, audiences, and the work itself while keeping moderation understandable.

1. Publish work you have the right to publish

Do not upload stolen art, repost another creator’s work as your own, or publish material you do not have permission to distribute. Fan work and transformative work remain the creator’s responsibility to publish lawfully.

2. Label mature or sensitive work

Use mature-content and content-note controls where relevant. The goal is informed choice, not punishment for adult themes.

3. No targeted abuse

Harassment, doxxing, threats, hateful targeting, or coordinated abuse are not welcome. Critique of work is allowed; campaigns against people are not.

4. No exploitative or illegal sexual content

Sexual content involving minors, non-consensual sexual material, or illegal exploitative imagery is prohibited.

5. Reporting is reviewed, not weaponised

A report creates a moderation review. It does not automatically remove work. Repeated bad-faith reporting may itself be actioned.

For the current public MVP, moderation actions and reports are represented locally in the browser. Server-side review, appeals, and enforcement history are part of the production backend path.
