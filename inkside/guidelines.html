INKSIDE
Community & safety

Make boldly.
Don’t harm people.

INKSIDE exists for independent art and comics. We want creators to take creative risks while keeping the platform safe, lawful and useful to readers.

Welcome here

  • Original art, comics, illustration and visual storytelling.
  • Fan work where the creator has the rights or permission needed to publish it.
  • Mature themes when they are legal, properly labelled and not exploitative.
  • Criticism, parody and difficult ideas expressed without targeted abuse.

Not allowed

  • Sexual content involving minors or anyone presented as a minor.
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery, doxxing, credible threats or targeted harassment.
  • Content that promotes or facilitates violent extremist activity.
  • Impersonation, fraud, malware, spam or deceptive commercial activity.
  • Work uploaded without the rights required to publish it.

Copyright and ownership

Creators keep ownership of their work. Publishing on INKSIDE does not transfer copyright to INKSIDE. Uploaders must have the rights needed to display and distribute what they publish.

Enforcement

Reports can be queued for review. Depending on severity and evidence, action can include labelling, reduced discovery, removal, account restrictions or escalation where legally required. We aim to distinguish good-faith artistic expression from abuse.

Report something

Use the Report action on any work in the app. The current preview stores the report locally as a moderation-queue event; production will route it to a review system.
