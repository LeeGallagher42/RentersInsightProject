Creator terms · launch principles

Your work stays yours.

These launch principles explain the product promise INKSIDE is being built around. They are intentionally plain-language and will be replaced by formal legal terms before real-money transactions are enabled.

90 / 10

Creators keep 90% of eligible platform revenue attributed to their paid work. INKSIDE keeps 10%. Payment processor fees, taxes, refunds and chargebacks may reduce the amount available for the split and should be shown clearly before payout.

Ownership

You retain copyright and ownership of what you upload. You give INKSIDE only the limited permission needed to host, display, distribute and promote your work inside the service while it is published.

Pricing

Creators choose whether a work is free or paid and set the list price where paid publishing is supported. INKSIDE should not silently discount creator work without an explicit programme or creator opt-in.

Removal

Creators can remove their work from public discovery, subject to lawful retention, completed transactions and abuse-prevention requirements.

Transparency

The product should make the creator share visible wherever money is discussed: publishing, checkout, creator studio and payout reporting.
